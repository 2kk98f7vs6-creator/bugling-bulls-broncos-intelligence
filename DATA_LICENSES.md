# Public NFL data attribution and sharing

The dashboard calculates and displays play-level statistics sourced from:

- **nflverse and nflreadpy**: https://github.com/nflverse/nflreadpy
  Most nflverse datasets have CC BY 4.0 licensing. Credit nflverse.
- **FTN Data via nflverse**: https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/reference/load_participation.html
  Participation for seasons 2023 onward and FTN charting use CC BY-SA 4.0.
  Attribute FTN Data via nflverse. Derivative data derived from these fields
  is subject to applicable share-alike requirements.
- **NFL Next Gen Stats via nflverse**:
  https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_nextgen_stats.html
  Only publicly distributed, week-level fields are incorporated.

Do not upload paywalled analyst articles, videos or proprietary tracking data to
GitHub without permission. Expert film review is a future, separately licensed
feature. No automated scraping of analysts has been implemented.

This independent fan prototype is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
Denver Broncos or the National Football League.
